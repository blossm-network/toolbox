The unscoped legacy `merkle-patricia-tree`, `ethereumjs-util` and `rlp` names are already at their latest releases. A switch to newer EthereumJS namespaces is a historical proof/encoding migration, not a same-name security patch. Preserve roots, encoded bytes and proof vectors before making that change. Function-level reachability matters: installed PBKDF2/advisory code is not proof that Blossm calls the affected key-derivation APIs.

A patched direct version does not prove every nested copy is patched. Final combined resolution is checked after all source owners land; known conditional or unmaintained transitive advisories must remain visible rather than being dismissed or silently forced across incompatible major ranges.
